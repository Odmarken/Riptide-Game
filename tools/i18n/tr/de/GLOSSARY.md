# German - terminology decisions (from the terms pass, 2026-10-13)

The full glossary is terms-01..04.json in this folder; these are the decisions behind it.

1. Address the player as "du", with du-imperatives. In ALL CAPS, ß becomes SS (GROSSER GEWINN).
2. Prestige stays "das Prestige" (Prestige 10, ab Prestige 5). Level = "Stufe", abbreviated "St.". XP = EP, HP = LP, MP stays MP, ATK/CRIT = ANG/KRIT.
3. Tides (the creatures) = "die Tide / die Tiden". Compounds: Tidengilde (Tides Guild), Tidenbank (Tides Bank), Tidenkampf, Tidenzucht, Tidenlager, Tidenmeister, Tidenbegleiter. Companion = "Begleiter". Species and moves are translated (Mondeule, Obsidianbär).
4. Crown Ledger = "das Hauptbuch der Krone", ledger = "Hauptbuch". A close = "der Abschluss" (Every close = Pro Abschluss). Season = "die Saison", treasury "Schatzkammer", debt "Schulden", in the red "im Minus".
5. Gold ◉ stays "Gold". Scraps ⚙ = "Schrott", to scrap = "verschrotten", scrap pouch = "Schrottbeutel". The City market level "Scraps" (food) = "Reste".
6. Peerage = "Adelsstand": Ritter/Ritterin, Baron/Baronin, Vizegraf/Vizegräfin, Graf/Gräfin, Marquis/Marquise, Herzog/Herzogin, König/Königin, Kaiser/Kaiserin. Commoner = "Bürgerlich", patent of nobility "Adelsbrief". Margrave = "Markgraf". Lord, Lady, Sir and Jarl stay before names.
7. Classes: Krieger, Magier, Jäger, Priester. Races: Mensch, Zwerg, Ork, Untoter. Spell and talent names follow German WoW usage (Heldenhafter Stoß, Feuerball, Frostnova, Göttliche Pein, Machtwort: Schild, Pyroschlag).
8. Places: Die Stadt (City), Der Hafen (Harbour), Der Hof (Farm; Farm Store = Hofladen, Farm Level = Hofstufe), Ödland (Wasteland). Thronsaal, Der Altar, Die Krypten, Die Letzte Stunde (The Final Hour), Kuhlevel. Walhalla, Mjölnir, Westliches Reich, Westmeer.
9. Coined place names stay in English, including zone names built on a coined word: Willowmere Fields, Thornwood Glade, Sunscar Sands, Kraken's Rest, Port Meridian. Names made only of plain words are translated: Aschenmoor, Die Versunkene Krypta, Violette Hallen.
10. Kapitän Blackbeard. His ship the Black Tide = "die Schwarze Tide"; other ship names are translated (Die Purpurwitwe).
11. Council = "der Rat", the King's Hand "die Hand des Königs", regency "Regentschaft", jail "Kerker", works "(öffentliche) Bauten", goodwill "Wohlwollen".
12. Free Company = "Freie Kompanie", sellsword "Söldner". City Watch = "Stadtwache"; watchman "Wachmann", guardsman "Gardist". The Forsaken = "die Verlassenen", The Forsaken One = "Der Verlassene".
13. NPC titles translated and gendered by the name, the name untouched: Bäckerin Klara, Bruder Anselm, Fischweib Greta, Marktfrau Liv, Kaufmann, Matrose, Krämerin, Knappe, Fräulein.
14. chest = Truhe, case = Kiste, bag = Tasche, gear = Ausrüstung, equip/unequip = ausrüsten/ablegen; scroll = Schriftrolle, tier = Rang, rating = Wertung. Rarities: Gewöhnlich, Edel (Fine), Selten, Episch, Legendär; Ungewöhnlich for the Tides' Uncommon.
15. Crafting: Schmied (the sign reads Schmiede), forge = Schmiede, furnace = Schmelzofen, enchant = Verzauberung, enchanting = Verzauberkunst, Smaragd, Bergbau. Ranks: Lehrling, Geselle, Experte, Meister.
16. A boss raid stays "Raid"; a raid on a port = "Überfall". Dungeon stays "Dungeon". Mount = "Reittier" (to mount = aufsitzen), pet "Haustier", talent tree "Talentbaum".
17. Casino: spin = Drehen, free spins = Freispiele, the dealer = "Bank" ("Die Bank gewinnt."), Hit/Stand/Double = Karte/Stehen/Verdoppeln, "Nichts geht mehr", straight up = Plein, Ride the Bus = Busfahren.
18. Item names are joined word by word (prefix + base): prefixes masculine adjectives ("Glänzender"), the 12 bases masculine nouns (Säbel, Speer, Herrscherstab, Kürass, Siegelring, Olifant...). "Worn" = "Getragen" (also the outfit button).
19. "s are" = "n sind", for "Your next {0} case{1} free." written as "...Kiste{1} gratis.".
20. Code strings and ids (use strict, ArrowDown, P mE, grayscale(...), träd) and the old legendary names (Frostmourne, The One Ring) map to themselves.
