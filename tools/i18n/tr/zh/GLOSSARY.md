# Simplified Chinese - terminology decisions (from the terms pass, 2026-10-13)

The full glossary is terms-01..04.json in this folder; these are the decisions behind it. Address the player as 你.

1. The creatures Tide/Tides = 潮灵 (Tides Guild 潮灵公会, Tide battle 潮灵对战). Sea-tide names stay literal: Tides Bank 潮汐银行, The Black Tide 黑潮号, Tidekeeper 守潮人.
2. Prestige = 威望 ("P10" also written 威望 10). Rating = 积分. Level = 等级 or "{n} 级"; the HUD keeps "Lv {n}".
3. Gold ◉ = 金币. Scraps ⚙ = 废料; to scrap gear = 分解; scrap pouch = 废料袋. The food budget level "Scraps" = 残羹剩饭.
4. Crown Ledger = 王室账簿, Ledger = 账簿. The Crown as an institution = 王室 (Crown rents 王室地租); the crown as an object or kingship = 王冠 (take the crown 夺取王冠). Royal = 皇家.
5. Season = 季度; a close = 结算; treasury = 国库; strongroom = 金库; bank vault = 保险库.
6. Peerage = 爵位: Commoner 平民, Knight/Dame 骑士/女骑士, Baron/Baroness 男爵/女男爵, Viscount 子爵/女子爵, Count 伯爵/女伯爵, Marquess/Marchioness 侯爵/女侯爵, Duke/Duchess 公爵/女公爵, King/Queen 国王/女王, Emperor/Empress 皇帝/女皇. Noble NPC wives are 男爵夫人 and 伯爵夫人.
7. Classes 战士/法师/猎人/牧师; races 人类/矮人/兽人/亡灵 - bare words (the game joins "Human Warrior" into 人类战士).
8. City = 王城; Harbour and Port = 港口; Throne Hall 王座大厅; Wasteland 荒原; Farm 农场; Cow Level 奶牛关; The Altar 祭坛; The Final Hour 终焉之时; The Crypts 地穴; Violet Halls 紫罗兰殿堂; Valhalla 英灵殿; Western Realm 西境.
9. Invented place names stay whole in Latin letters: Moonshine, Silverfjord, Port Meridian, Kraken’s Rest, Willowmere Fields, Mistfen Marsh, Sunscar Sands and the like. Names built only from ordinary words are translated: Ashen Moor 灰烬沼地, The Sunken Crypt 沉没地穴. Buildings named after a place: "Moonshine 旅店", "Riptide 赌场", "Silverfjord 宫殿".
10. People's names stay in Latin letters, with a half-width space next to Chinese. Jobs before the name (铁匠 Garrick, 城卫 Nils); ranks and honorifics after (Ulf Gyllenklo 男爵, Ansgar 修士, Agnes 修女, Jasper 勋爵, Ilse 夫人, Folke 雅尔). Ship captains 船长, the mercenary captain 队长 (Hakon Stormgaard 队长). Nicknames translated, in front: 独眼 Sixten, 根魔 Gorehusk, 军阀 Krev.
11. Odin 奥丁, Thor 托尔, Mjolnir 雷神之锤, Captain Blackbeard 黑胡子船长, The One Ring 至尊魔戒, The Ring 魔戒. Frostseeker stays in Latin letters.
12. Council 议会 (chamber 议事厅), the Hand 首相, regency 摄政, the player as steward 总管, Free Company 自由佣兵团, sellsword 佣兵. The watch 城卫队, a watchman 城卫, cadets 见习兵. Heralds 纹章官, patent 特许状, public works 公共工程 (Works 工程), unrest 动乱, goodwill 好感, allies 盟友. The Forsaken 被遗弃者, The Forsaken One 被遗弃之人.
13. Boss 首领, dungeon 地下城. Group raids like Violet Halls = 团队副本; raids on ports = 突袭. Hardcore 硬核, talent 天赋, Skill Tree 技能树.
14. Rarity: Common 普通, Fine 精良, Uncommon 优秀, Rare 稀有, Epic 史诗, Legendary 传说, Mutant 变异. Spectral 幽灵, Hybrid 混种.
15. Enchant 附魔, emerald 翡翠, scroll 卷轴 (Tier II = II 阶), chest or case 宝箱, companion 伙伴, pet 宠物, mount 坐骑, outfit 服装, Bag 背包. Spell and talent names follow the official Chinese World of Warcraft names (火球术, 寒冰箭, 真言术：盾).
16. Casino: slots 老虎机, blackjack 二十一点 (a blackjack hand 黑杰克), roulette 轮盘, Ride the Bus 搭巴士, jackpot 头奖, BIG/MEGA/MAX WIN 大奖/巨奖/最高奖, free spins 免费旋转. GAMBAAA! 搏一把！, GOLD GOLD GOLD 金金金.
17. Chinese punctuation ，。！？： in Chinese text. Keep " - " separators, emoji and ◉ ⚙ · …. Button hints stay half-width "(X)". MAX, Lv, LB and RB stay.
18. "Worn" = 破旧 (the item prefix; it also labels the worn outfit). "s are" (in "Your next {0} case{1} free.") = 均.
